Our Services

Every piece is handcrafted with precision — from the first sketch to the final finish.

Custom Wood Furniture

Beds, sofas frames, dining tables, study desks — any furniture built to your exact dimensions and taste using premium hardwood.

Windows

Wooden window frames, casement windows, sliding sash designs — crafted for durability, ventilation, and a polished aesthetic.

Doors

Main entrance doors, interior panel doors, and frame fitting — solid wood or engineered, finished with lacquer or varnish.

Interior Carpentry

False ceilings, wall panelling, TV units, loft beds, and all bespoke woodwork to transform any interior space.

Pooja Room Unit

Beautifully crafted wooden pooja mandirs with intricate carvings, shelving, and doors — built with reverence and fine detail.

Furniture Repairs

Broken joints, worn surfaces, damaged panels — we restore your existing furniture to its original strength and beauty at affordable rates.

Cupboards

Built-in and freestanding cupboards for kitchens, bedrooms, and living rooms — designed for maximum storage and a clean, lasting finish.

POP Ceiling

Elegant Plaster of Paris false ceiling designs — from simple borders to layered coves and recessed lighting panels for every room.
